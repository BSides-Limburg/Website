About BSides Limburg

By the community,
for the community

BSides Limburg is part of Security BSides, a worldwide network of grassroots security conferences that started in Las Vegas in 2009. Every BSides is organised locally, by volunteers, with an open call for papers.

We bring that spirit to Limburg: a place where students, researchers, pentesters, blue teamers and the curious meet. Talks are in English, the atmosphere is informal and the coffee keeps flowing.

FAQ

Good
questions

Something missing? Mail info@bsides-limburg.be.

Why do tickets cost money?

A small fee keeps no-shows down, so we can plan catering and seats properly. BSides Limburg is non-profit: any surplus goes to the next edition and to charity. Late Decider tickets cost a bit more.

In which language are the talks?

All talks are in English.

Is there WiFi?

Yes, Corda Campus offers a guest network. It is a shared, production network, so please don't hack it or put it at risk. Save that energy for the CTF.

Will there be photos and video?

Yes, our official crew takes photos and video. Official media may be freely used by the community. Commercial photography needs consent from everyone involved and approval from the organisers. See the Code of Conduct.

Can I buy a T-shirt separately?

There's no swag shop. T-shirts come with Early Bird and Normal tickets only.

How do I get there?

Hasselt-Kiewit station is a 5-minute walk. See the venue section for bus lines and car access.
