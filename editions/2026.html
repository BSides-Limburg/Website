Workshop · Thursday 12 March 2026

BlackHoodie
workshop

"Introduction to reverse engineering and binary exploitation", a free full-day workshop (09:00–17:00) primarily for women and gender-diverse participants, including lunch and a ticket for the conference day.

About the workshop

Trained by Paula M. (pamoutaf) and supported by BlackHoodie and CybHerStrong. Prerequisites: basic C and Python, comfort on the Linux command line and a laptop with Docker.
