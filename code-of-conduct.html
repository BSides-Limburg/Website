Code of Conduct

Be excellent
to each other

BSides Limburg is dedicated to a harassment-free conference experience for everyone. This applies to talks, workshops, the CTF, the afterparty and our online channels.

The short version

We are dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion (or lack thereof), or technology choices.

We do not tolerate harassment of participants in any form. Sexual language and imagery is not appropriate at any conference venue, including talks, workshops, parties and online media. Anyone violating these rules may be sanctioned or expelled from the conference, without a refund, at the discretion of the organisers.

The longer version

Harassment includes, but is not limited to:

  • Offensive verbal comments related to the characteristics listed above
  • Sexual images in public spaces or in presentations
  • Deliberate intimidation, stalking or following
  • Harassing photography or recording
  • Sustained disruption of talks or other events
  • Inappropriate physical contact or unwelcome sexual attention
  • Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above

Participants asked to stop any harassing behaviour are expected to comply immediately. This applies equally to speakers, sponsors, volunteers and organisers. Sponsors should not use sexualised images, activities or other material, and booth staff should not use sexualised clothing or uniforms.

If a participant engages in harassing behaviour, the organisers may take any action they deem appropriate, from a warning to expulsion from the conference.

Photo & video policy

Our official crew takes photos and video during the event, and these are a priority for us. Official BSides Limburg media may be freely used by the community.

Commercial photography or filming of the event, its participants or its grounds requires consent from everyone involved and prior approval from the organisers. Always respect people who ask not to be photographed.

Hacking etiquette

  • The venue WiFi is a shared, production network. Do not attack it or put it at risk.
  • Keep attacks inside the CTF scope. Do not target other participants, their devices or the venue's infrastructure.
  • Respect responsible disclosure when talking about vulnerabilities.

Reporting

If you are being harassed, notice that someone else is being harassed, or have any other concerns, please contact an organiser or volunteer immediately. They are easy to recognise by their staff T-shirts.

Prefer to write? Mail info@bsides-limburg.be. Every report is handled confidentially.

Our team will help participants contact venue security or local law enforcement, provide escorts, or otherwise help those experiencing harassment feel safe for the duration of the conference. We value your attendance.
